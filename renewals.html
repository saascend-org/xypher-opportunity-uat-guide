Auto-renewal

Renewals appear on their own the day a contract enters its window. What arrives, what is deliberately missing, and the one question that needs a decision from the business.

What happens without you doing anything

A job runs daily at 06:00. When a won contract enters its renewal window it creates a renewal opportunity, posts guidance on it, and marks the original so it is never processed twice.

The renewal arrives at Qualify, owned by you if you owned the original and are still active, with contract dates rolled forward and a next step already set. It has no products — deliberately. Last year’s prices are last year’s prices.

Plain renewal versus expansion

A plain renewal walks through Qualify and Solution Validation with almost nothing required. Tick Renewal Includes Expansion and the full qualification gates apply at every stage. Tick it when the scope has genuinely grown; leave it when it has not.
