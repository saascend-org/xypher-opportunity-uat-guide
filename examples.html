Watch out

Ticking the box re-opens every qualification gate. That is intentional — extra scope is a new sale with a warm start, not an admin renewal.

Naming

Use one convention so pipeline reports group properly. Consistency matters more than the exact format:

Customer - Service - Year

So: Northshire CC - Pen Testing 2027. Renewals the system creates are named for you and already follow it.

Which Type should I pick?

TypeUse it whenWhat it changes
New BusinessA service this customer does not buy todayFull qualification at every gate
Cross-SellA different service line into an existing customerFull qualification — it is a new sale to a warm account
ExpansionMore of what they already buySolution Validation is light-touch: Economic Buyer and Champion only
RenewalSame scope, new termQualification gates step aside — unless you tick Renewal Includes Expansion

The honest test

If you are asking the buyer for money they have not budgeted for, it is not a renewal — whatever the paperwork calls it.
